# Commerce Try-On

Merchants register Product/Material/Pattern/Geometry assets. Products can be tried on individually or assembled into a composite Look.

## Product asset contract

- target body region
- product category
- visual assets
- semantic attributes
- geometry/measurement metadata where available
- material metadata where available
- evidence tier
- allowed runtimes

## Outcome loop

`view → try-on → click → cart → purchase → return/fit outcome → reality calibration`

Returns, satisfaction, alteration and fit events can become evidence, subject to consent and merchant/user agreements.
