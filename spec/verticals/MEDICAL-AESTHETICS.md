# Medical-Aesthetic Simulation Boundary

Plastic surgery and related medical-aesthetic procedures are not ordinary consumer Looks.

## Required controls

- explicit mode selection
- procedure description
- licensed-clinician attribution where applicable
- simulation vs outcome disclosure
- evidence tier
- consent capture
- audit trail
- anatomical plausibility checks where supported
- no guarantee of surgical outcome
- no automatic treatment/diagnosis claims

## Safety objective

Prevent both misleading certainty and implausible/cartoonish transformations. Unsupported requests may be downgraded, blocked or surfaced as exploratory only.
