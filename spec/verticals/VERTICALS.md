# Vertical Strategy

## Initial launch verticals

### Makeup
Region-aware operations for base, contour, blush, eyes, brows, lashes and lips.

### Hair / Barbering
Cut, silhouette, volume, part, texture and colour operations.

### Fashion / Garments
Garment transfer, material/colour representation, silhouette and evolving fit tiers.

## Expansion verticals

Hands, nails, jewelry, accessories, footwear, skin aesthetics, tailoring, fashion design and composite styling.

## Medical aesthetics
Separate module with stricter evidence, disclaimers, consent and regulatory handling.
