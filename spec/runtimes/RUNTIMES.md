# LooksWise Runtime Model

## Live AR
Low-latency, bounded effects. Prefer device perception/tracking and lightweight compositing. No guarantee of high-fidelity output.

## Photo
Higher-fidelity semantic transformation with constraint and provenance verification.

## Video
Temporal tracking/memory and frame-consistency verification are first-class.

## High fidelity
May use expensive specialist capability implementations and deeper evaluation.

## External distribution
A Look may be compiled into compatible effect/distribution packages where a target platform permits it. Do not assume a universal external filter API.
