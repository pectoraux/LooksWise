# Social Validation

## Why it exists

The product recognizes that people frequently validate appearance choices socially. LooksWise therefore separates **private simulation** from **public or controlled-opinion validation**.

## Social Look Post

A post references:

- source media
- Look version
- transformation result
- author
- visibility policy
- opinion audience policy
- engagement counters
- moderation status
- external-share status

## Audience controls

The author can choose an allowed opinion audience. A gender-based audience restriction is an optional control subject to legal/product policy. It must not be stored as a claim about whose judgment is objectively valid.

## Integrity controls

- one-vote-per-user-per-post semantics
- rate limiting
- bot/spam detection
- brigading detection
- privacy-safe aggregation
- block/mute/report
- moderation queues
- creator controls for comments

## External sharing

Shares to external platforms are tracked as distribution events, not as controlled in-app opinion. The exported media/link should disclose that external engagement may not be governed by LooksWise.
